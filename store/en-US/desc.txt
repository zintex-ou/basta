Open your phone for one thing and lose 40 minutes to social media? Basta stops it at the root.

Basta blocks the apps that distract you exactly when you need to focus, and gives you back the time endless scrolling takes every day.

HOW IT WORKS
• Pick your distracting apps: social media, video, games or websites.
• Create focus modes: Work, Study, Sleep, Gym… as many as you like.
• Turn them on by schedule: Mon–Fri 9 to 5, or every night from 11 pm.
• Turn them on by place: arrive at the office, library or home and they lock automatically.
• Quick focus: one tap for 15, 30, 60 or 120 minutes without distractions.

STRICT MODE
Serious about it? Turn on strict mode and you can't switch the block off until the session ends. No loopholes.

YOUR SCREEN TIME, CLEAR
See how much time you've spent on your phone today and which apps took most of it.

TOTAL PRIVACY
Basta uses Apple's Screen Time. Everything stays on your iPhone — we never see which apps you use or where you are.

SUBSCRIPTION
Basta requires a subscription to use all features. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings.
